lmnop

One document, and every way of looking at it.

A mindmap and a document are not two files that have to be kept in step. They are two ways of seeing the same thing. lmnop keeps one document and lets you look at it as a map, a page, a board, a whiteboard, a timeline — and change it from any of them.

What it is for

Thinking, not filing

The point of notes is the model you end up holding in your head. lmnop is built at the scale where the structure of an idea still fits on a screen — and stays visible while you work on it.

Yours, on your machine

Everything is stored locally first, so it opens instantly and keeps working with no connection. When there is one, your devices catch each other up.

In a room with people

Workshop mode runs a live transcript beside the map, so a conversation and the structure it is producing are on screen at the same time.
